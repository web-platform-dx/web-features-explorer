Opposed features

The following features have received negative standards positions from at least one browser vendor. They're very unlikely to ever become Baseline in their current forms.

  • Accelerometer

    Limited availability

    The Accelerometer, LinearAccelerationSensor and GravitySensor APIs read the acceleration applied to a device in three dimensions, either including the effect of gravity, without its effect, or only its effect, respectively.

    Learn more

    • Chrome 91 Released on 2021-05-25
    • Chrome Android 91 Released on 2021-05-25
    • Edge 91 Released on 2021-05-27
    • Firefox Not supported
    • Firefox for Android Not supported

    • Safari on iOS Not supported Position: oppose

    Limited availability
