<bdo>

Widely Available (since 2018-01-29)

The <bdo> element overrides the directionality of text, so that the text within is rendered in a different direction than the surrounding text.

Browser support

  • Chrome ≤15 Released before 2011-10-25
  • Chrome Android 18 Released on 2012-06-27
  • Edge 12 Released on 2015-07-29
  • Firefox 10 Released on 2012-01-31
  • Firefox for Android 10 Released on 2012-01-31
  • Safari ≤4 Released before 2009-06-08
  • Safari on iOS ≤3.2 Released before 2010-04-03
